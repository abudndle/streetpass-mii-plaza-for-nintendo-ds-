## Experimental SPDR radio integration

DSWifi now transports validated 80-byte SPDR frames with a two-hop budget rather than raw 64-byte SPDS. The receiver uses the replay cache and CRC before persisting encounters. Forwarding is deliberately disabled until explicit consent controls and airtime scheduling are tested. The 3DS native StreetPass protocol is not supported. The app must remain running; SD files cannot start background processes in other games.
